Kvittbox

Receipt & Warranty Keeper for Android

Kvittbox keeps your receipts on your phone and reminds you before a return window or a warranty ends.

What it does

  • Scan a paper receipt, pick a screenshot, import a PDF, share one from another app, or type it in.
  • Reads the store, date, total and currency on your phone. No internet needed.
  • Return and warranty dates per receipt or per item, with reminders before they end and on the last day.
  • "Ending soon" home screen widget, search, categories, and a claim PDF for warranty claims.
  • Optional app lock with your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • In 13 languages.

Private by design

No account, no server, no ads, no trackers. Your receipts stay on your phone. The first 10 receipts are free; a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited receipts. No subscription.

Why Kvittbox asks for Google Drive access

Only if you turn on the optional backup. Kvittbox then saves one encrypted backup file in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). The file is locked with your password on your phone before it is uploaded. Kvittbox can't see your other Drive files, and we never receive your backup.
